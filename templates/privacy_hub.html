Privacy Hub

Quick, practical privacy knowledge for everyday digital life.

🔐

Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords for important accounts. Avoid using the same password everywhere.

🎣

Phishing

Check unexpected messages, senders and links before clicking or responding.

📱

App Permissions

Only give applications permissions they actually need. Review access regularly.

💳

Payment Safety

Never share your OTP, PIN or banking credentials. Verify payment requests independently.

🌐

Social Privacy

Review who can see your profile, posts, location and other personal information.

🧠

Think Before You Share

Before providing personal information online, ask whether the service really needs it.
